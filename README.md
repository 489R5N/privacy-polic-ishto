## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Personal Data Provided by You
* **Account Information:** Display name / username and email address when you create an account or sign in.
* **Authentication Providers:**
  * Google Sign-In
  * Google Play Games Services
  * Email & Password
  * Guest Mode (generates a random, anonymous local identifier)
* **Passwords:** Passwords are processed securely by Firebase Authentication; we never see or store raw passwords.

### B. Gameplay and Economy Data
* In-game coins balance, cosmetics inventory (dice skins, boards, pawns), pass tiers, and Lucky Spin rewards.
* Match history, win/loss statistics, and achievements.

### C. Technical and Device Information
* Device model, operating system version, and general network connectivity status.
* Crash logs and diagnostic data to improve game stability.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information
* **Game Operations:** To create online multiplayer rooms, manage turns, validate game state, and prevent cheating.
* **Account Sync:** To restore your unlocked cosmetic items, coin balances, and purchase entitlements across devices.
* **Customer Support:** To assist you with account inquiries or purchase restoration.
* **Service Protection:** To protect the economy, enforce fair play, and comply with legal requirements.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services & SDKs
ISHTO integrates trusted third-party services:
* **Google Play Games Services:** For authentication, achievements, and player identity.
* **Google Play Billing:** For secure in-app purchases and subscriptions/passes.
* **Google Firebase (Auth, Firestore, Realtime Database):** For player login, cloud storage, and real-time multiplayer syncing.
* **Google AdMob:** For rewarded videos and post-game interstitial ads.

---

## 4. In-App Purchases & Virtual Economy
* Virtual coins and cosmetic items are limited, revocable in-game licenses with no real-world monetary value.
* All financial transactions are securely handled directly by Google Play. We do not store or process your credit card, debit card, or banking information.

## 5. Advertising & Advertising ID
* **Rewarded Ads:** You may optionally watch video ads to earn bonus coins or rewards.
* **Interstitial Ads:** Limited interstitial ads may appear after completing local matches. Ads are never displayed mid-game.
* **Advertising ID (AAID):** Used by Google AdMob in accordance with Google Play Developer Policies for ad frequency capping, fraud prevention, and analytics.

---

## 6. Data Storage & Security
* Local game preferences and cache are stored securely in Android app storage.
* Online data is transferred using industry-standard TLS encryption.
* Server-authoritative validation prevents unauthorized modifications to game state or player currencies.

---

## 7. Account & Data Deletion
You retain complete control over your data:
1. **In-App Deletion:** You can delete your account directly inside ISHTO by opening **Settings ➔ Account ➔ Delete Account**.
2. **Email Request:** You can email us at **pixelcube.dev@gmail.com** with your username/email to request permanent deletion of your account and associated game data.
3. Upon deletion, personal account identifiers and cloud game saves are permanently purged.

## 8. Children's Privacy
