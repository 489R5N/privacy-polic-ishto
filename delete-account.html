ISHTO - Account & Data Deletion

In accordance with Google Play Data Safety policies, players can request complete deletion of their ISHTO account and associated gameplay data.

Option 1: In-App Instant Deletion

You can delete your account and local data immediately inside the game:

  1. Open the ISHTO app.
  2. Tap Settings ⚙️ from the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Tap "Account & Data" → "Delete Account" / "Reset All Data".
  4. Confirm deletion. Your account, progress, and cloud data will be wiped immediately.

Option 2: Web / Email Deletion Request

If you have uninstalled the app or wish to request manual deletion from our servers, send an email to our support team:

  • Data Deleted: Profile username, player avatar, Firebase Auth UID, online game match history, coins balance, and unlock records.
  • Data Retained: In-app purchase transaction receipts may be retained as required by tax and financial compliance laws.
  • Processing Time: Requests are processed within 7 business days.
